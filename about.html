The Concept

What This Archive Is

Haus of Memories preserves moments, places, objects, marks, and visual culture that may otherwise disappear.

The photograph preserves what the physical world may erase. A tag can be painted over. A train can leave. A wall can be demolished. A neighborhood can change. But the photograph becomes a record that survives.

This is an archive, not a photography store. Every image here is a personal photograph — taken primarily on iPhone, treated as archival source material rather than raw material for editing. Original aspect ratios are preserved. Nothing is unnecessarily cropped, stretched, distorted, or recolored.

The first major collection documents graffiti, tagging, trains, walls, street markings, abandoned spaces, and other found visual moments. But graffiti is one collection within a larger project, not the whole of it — the broader subject is visual memory.

A Note

Documentation, Not Exposure

This project documents visual culture — it does not identify graffiti artists, expose private information, or encourage illegal activity. Locations are recorded generally (city or area), never as exact coordinates, and are never made more specific than that without the photographer's explicit choice.

The archive is about documentation, preservation, authorship, place, memory, and visual culture — nothing more.
